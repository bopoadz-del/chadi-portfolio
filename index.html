The Machinery

The Blocks, The Kits, The Agents

THE BLOCK
One contract, certified once

Every capability — retrieval, agents, documents, vision, engineering formulas — behind one uniform contract: declared inputs and outputs, validated at runtime, certified once and reused everywhere.

THE KIT
A swappable domain container

A kit is a manifest of block references plus domain configuration — construction, retail, hospitality, estate operations. Swap the kit, keep the machinery. A new domain is a new manifest, not a new codebase.

THE AGENT + HAT
One runtime, department hats

Each department is an agent wearing a governed hat: knowledge boundaries, confirmation-gated memory, defined handoffs, and playbooks carrying the formulas, standards and procedures of the trade.
